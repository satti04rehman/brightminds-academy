FAQ

Before you ask. The answers.

How much are the monthly fees?

Fees range from Rs. 3,500 to Rs. 7,500 per month depending on the program. Fees include all study material and monthly reports. Paying a term in advance (3 months) saves 5%, and siblings enjoy a 10% discount for the second child.

How large are the batches?

Maximum 8 students per batch — a guarantee we never break. This keeps classes interactive and gives every child individual attention, homework review and doubt-clearing time.

Is the trial class really free?

Yes, completely free and with no obligation. You attend one full class with your child's prospective batch, meet the mentor, and then decide. No registration fee is charged at any point.

When do new batches begin?

Every month. We also run mid-month intakes whenever a seat opens. Tell us your preferred timing and we will match you with the next available batch.

What is the refund policy?

Fees are charged monthly. If you leave before the month ends, the remaining classes are adjusted — either refunded or carried to the next month. There is no security deposit and no fine for leaving.

Do you guide on board or study choices?

Yes. As part of Matric/FSc and O/A Level programs, our mentors offer free career counselling sessions — subject selection, stream choices and realistic university pathways.
